Every radio amateur has a drawer full of antenna insulators of all shapes and sizes.

In Poland, surplus military porcelain insulators were the most popular for many years. They used to be available by the kilo, but nowadays they are either expensive or impossible to find.

Fortunately, 3D printers have become so widespread that almost every DIY enthusiast has one of their own :-)

I designed these insulators in my favourite tool, OpenSCAD. The great thing about this design is that you can simply enter your wire diameter and the insulator will automatically resize itself to match.
