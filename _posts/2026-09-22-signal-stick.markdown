This is probably the simplest and quite an effective antenna you can make for your handheld radio. It is simply a quarter-wave length piece of wire for the 2-meter band, which operates on the 70 cm band on the third harmonic. You will need an SMA connector for an RG58 cable (with a wider flange https://allegro.pl/listing?string=sma%20rg58 ), 50 cm of NITINOL wire – a nickel-titanium shape-memory alloy that allows the wire to return to its original form even after being tied in a knot :-) – and some heat shrink tubing (I used a glow-in-the-dark one because it looks awesome).

1. Solder the wire to the center pin of the connector.
2. Put the heat shrink tubing over the soldered joint and shrink it.
3. Insert the finished element into the housing; for greater durability, add a little bit of two-part epoxy glue.
4. Put on the 3D-printed cap.
5. Enjoy your great antenna!

You can find the 3D printing files here: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6810945
