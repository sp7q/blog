Many people have been asking me about this paddle because it looks interesting, mainly because I've used some fancy shirt buttons! :-)

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RtEYI4aF7j4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RtEYI4aF7j4)

It's really easy to make, and there's a good chance you already have all the parts you need in your drawer.

You'll need:
- paper clip
- binder clip
- metal shirt buttons - solderable
- some plastic main body element - in my case a random plastic pushbutton
- magnet
- 2 screws
- piece of PCB

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/clip1.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="clip1" width="600"> 

First, you need to prepare the binder clip.

File down the edge so that the binder clip handle can be pushed out.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/clip2.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="clip2" width="600">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/clip3.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="clip3" width="600">


Now you can put everything together:

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/clip4.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="clip4" width="600">

Use the paper clip to make arm limiters.

Solder the buttons to the binder clip handles.

Glue the PCB to the binder clip. These will act as the paddle contacts.

And there you have it — a cheap but surprisingly nice, fully functional paddle! :-)
